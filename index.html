Frequently Asked Questions

Questions You May Have

Here are answers to some common questions about our services.

How do I request a service?
Select a department and service using the enquiry form. Provide your details and continue to WhatsApp to discuss your request.
How much do your services cost?
Pricing depends on the service and the details of your request. Contact the appropriate department for a quote before confirming.
Can I request more than one service?
Yes. You can contact the relevant departments separately through WhatsApp to discuss different services.
Can I enquire about an appliance that is not listed?
Yes. Use the Custom Request option or contact the Home Appliances department. Product availability and pricing must be confirmed.
Do you offer move-in and move-out cleaning?
These are available as enquiry options. Share your property type, location and cleaning requirements so the team can discuss availability and pricing.
Does submitting the form confirm my booking?
No. The form prepares a WhatsApp enquiry. Your booking is not confirmed until the service details and availability have been discussed with the appropriate department.
How can I leave a rating or feedback?
Visit the Ratings & Feedback section, choose the service, select your star rating, write your feedback and continue to WhatsApp.
